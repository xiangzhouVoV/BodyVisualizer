THE BASICS

Why Count Macros Instead of Calories?

Calories tell you how much to eat. Macros tell you what to eat. A macro calculator breaks your daily intake into protein, carbs and fat — so you know your body is getting the right fuel, not just the right number. Whether your goal is weight loss, muscle gain, or maintenance, tracking macros gives you an actionable plan.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much protein do I need per day?

Most adults need 1.6 to 2.2 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight, depending on your activity level and goal. Our calculator uses your body weight and goal to give you a personalized number.

How many carbs should I eat per day?

Carb intake depends on your remaining calories after protein and fat are accounted for. For most people, carbs make up 40 to 60 percent of daily calories. Enter your stats above and our calculator does the math for you.

What is a macro calculator?

A macro calculator estimates how many grams of protein, carbohydrates and fat you should eat each day, based on your body stats, activity level and fitness goal. It turns your calorie target into an actionable eating plan.

Is tracking macros better than counting calories?

Counting calories helps you control how much you eat. Tracking macros also controls what you eat. For body composition goals like building muscle or losing fat while keeping muscle, macros give you more precise control.

Do I need to track macros every day?

Not necessarily. Once you use our calculator a few times and learn what a typical day of eating looks like for your goals, you can estimate without tracking every gram. Use it as a starting point and check back when your weight or goals change.

Female Body Shapes

Hourglass

Hourglass shapes have shoulders and hips that are roughly equal in width, with a clearly defined waist. A waist-to-hip ratio is often below 0.75. This balanced proportion can be visualised from every angle, but it is a descriptive category rather than a rule that every person must fit.

How to tell: Compare shoulder and hip measurements, then check whether the waist is about 25% smaller than the hips.

Pear / Triangle

Pear, or triangle, shapes have hips that are wider than the shoulders and upper body. The waist may be defined while visual weight is carried lower on the body. Measurements help show the relationship between shoulder, waist, and hip proportions without assigning a value or health judgement.

How to tell: Your hip measurement is noticeably larger than your shoulder or bust measurement, often by around 5% or more.

Inverted Triangle

Inverted triangle shapes have shoulders that are visibly broader than the hips. The upper body can read as stronger or wider while the waist and lower body appear comparatively narrow. This calculator uses the relative shoulder-to-hip measurement, not weight or BMI, to identify that proportion.

How to tell: Your shoulder measurement is noticeably larger than your hip measurement, often by around 5% or more.

Apple / Round

Apple, or round, shapes tend to carry more visible proportion through the waist and midsection. The shoulders and hips can be more similar in size while the waist is less sharply defined. This is a simple visual category and does not describe body fat percentage, health, or a diagnosis.

How to tell: Your waist is close to your hip and upper-body measurements, so the middle is not sharply narrower.

Rectangle / Straight

Rectangle, or straight, shapes have shoulders, waist, and hips that remain relatively close in proportion. The silhouette may show fewer dramatic curves through the waist. It is a common body-shape pattern, and individual posture, muscle, clothing, and natural variation can still create very different appearances.

How to tell: Shoulder, waist, and hip measurements are relatively close, with less than roughly a 25% waist reduction from the hips.

Male Body Shapes

Trapezoid

Shoulders are slightly broader than the waist and hips, creating a naturally balanced upper body.

How to tell: Your shoulders are moderately wider than your waist, while hips stay close to your waist measurement.

Inverted Triangle

The shoulders and chest are noticeably wider than the waist and hips.

How to tell: Your shoulders or chest are substantially wider than both your waist and hips.

Rectangle

Shoulders, waist, and hips stay relatively close in width, creating a straighter silhouette.

How to tell: Shoulder, waist, and hip measurements sit fairly close together without a strong taper.

Oval

The waist and midsection carry more visible width than the shoulders and hips.

How to tell: Your waist is the widest or close to the widest measurement, creating more fullness through the middle.

Triangle

The waist and hips are wider than the shoulders, creating a broader lower silhouette.

How to tell: Your waist or hips are noticeably wider than your shoulders, creating a broader lower body.

How to Measure for Accurate Results

  1. Shoulders — measure around the widest point of your shoulders, keeping the tape level.
  2. Bust — measure around the fullest part of your chest.
  3. Waist — measure at the narrowest point, usually just above the belly button.
  4. Hips — measure around the widest part of your hips and buttocks.

Tip: keep the tape measure snug but not tight, and stand naturally. Once measured, enter the numbers above to see your 3D body shape.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between body shape and BMI?

BMI is a single number based on height and weight, while body shape describes your actual proportions. Two people can have the same BMI but completely different body shapes.

How accurate is the body shape calculator?

It uses standard shoulder-to-hip and waist-to-hip ratios to classify your shape. For the most accurate result, measure carefully with a soft tape measure.

What are the 5 main body shapes?

Hourglass, pear (triangle), inverted triangle, apple (round), and rectangle (straight).

What is the difference between body shape and body type?

Body shape describes visible shoulder, waist, and hip proportions, while body type is a broader phrase that may include frame, muscle, or styling context. Here, both terms refer to the same five proportion-based categories.

Do men have the same body shapes?

Men can also have hourglass, pear, inverted triangle, apple, or rectangle-like proportions, although typical measurements may differ.

What waist-to-hip ratio indicates each body shape?

A waist-to-hip ratio below about 0.75 often suggests a defined-waist hourglass pattern. Larger hip, shoulder, or waist ratios can suggest other categories, but these are visual guidelines rather than strict cutoffs.

The 5 Female Body Shapes

Hourglass

Hourglass shapes have shoulders and hips that are roughly equal in width, with a clearly defined waist. A waist-to-hip ratio is often below 0.75. This balanced proportion can be visualised from every angle, but it is a descriptive category rather than a rule that every person must fit.

Pear / Triangle

Pear, or triangle, shapes have hips that are wider than the shoulders and upper body. The waist may be defined while visual weight is carried lower on the body. Measurements help show the relationship between shoulder, waist, and hip proportions without assigning a value or health judgement.

Inverted Triangle

Inverted triangle shapes have shoulders that are visibly broader than the hips. The upper body can read as stronger or wider while the waist and lower body appear comparatively narrow. This calculator uses the relative shoulder-to-hip measurement, not weight or BMI, to identify that proportion.

Apple / Round

Apple, or round, shapes tend to carry more visible proportion through the waist and midsection. The shoulders and hips can be more similar in size while the waist is less sharply defined. This is a simple visual category and does not describe body fat percentage, health, or a diagnosis.

Rectangle / Straight

Rectangle, or straight, shapes have shoulders, waist, and hips that remain relatively close in proportion. The silhouette may show fewer dramatic curves through the waist. It is a common body-shape pattern, and individual posture, muscle, clothing, and natural variation can still create very different appearances.

How to Measure for Accurate Results

  1. Shoulders — measure around the widest point of your shoulders, keeping the tape level.
  2. Bust — measure around the fullest part of your chest.
  3. Waist — measure at the narrowest point, usually just above the belly button.
  4. Hips — measure around the widest part of your hips and buttocks.

Tip: keep the tape measure snug but not tight, and stand naturally. Once measured, enter the numbers above to see your 3D body shape.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between body shape and BMI?

BMI is a single number based on height and weight, while body shape describes your actual proportions. Two people can have the same BMI but completely different body shapes.

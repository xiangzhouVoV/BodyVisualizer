3. 页面结构（自上而下，单页滚动）
第一段：Hero 区（计算器工具本身）
打开页面立即可用，不需要点「开始」，没有弹窗挡路。

输入字段（一行排两个，移动端纵向堆叠）：

Gender：两个按钮切换，Male / Female
Age：数字输入，范围 10 到 120
Weight：数字输入，单位 kg，范围大于 0 且小于 500
Activity Level：下拉选择，五个选项（对应 Mifflin-St Jeor 活动系数）：
Sedentary (little or no exercise) — 系数 1.2
Lightly active (1-3 days/week) — 系数 1.375
Moderately active (3-5 days/week) — 系数 1.55
Very active (6-7 days/week) — 系数 1.725
Extra active (athlete, physical job) — 系数 1.9

Goal：下拉选择，三个选项：
Lose weight（热量缺口 400kcal）
Maintain weight（维持）
Build muscle（热量盈余 300kcal）

Country：下拉选择，选项从后端 GET /api/countries 动态拉取。前端加载时调一次，缓存到内存。
一个醒目的 Calculate 按钮。按钮上方或旁边一行小字：「Free, no sign-up required.」

第二段：结果区（计算完成后展示，初始隐藏）
结果区分两层：

第一层：每日宏量素 g 数（三栏并排，移动端纵向堆叠）

栏一：Protein

大字显示克数，如 140 g
小字换算：约 560 kcal（蛋白质每克 4kcal）
小字参考：相当于你体重的 2.0 g/kg
栏二：Carbs

大字显示克数，如 180 g
小字换算：约 720 kcal（碳水每克 4kcal）
栏三：Fat

大字显示克数，如 45 g
小字换算：约 405 kcal（脂肪每克 9kcal）
三栏下方一行总热量：Daily total: 约 XXXX kcal

第二层：每个宏量素下方的食物推荐（三栏各一个列表）

每栏列出该国家该类型的食物，每条展示：

食物名称（英文 + 当地名，如 Rice / 米饭）
每 100g 含该宏量素多少 g（从后端数据拿）
一份建议分量（默认值写在前端常量里，如米饭一份 = 200g，鸡胸肉一份 = 150g）
该分量能完成当日目标的百分比，如「200g 米饭 ≈ 当日碳水的 60%」
百分比计算逻辑（前端算）：食物每 100g 含量 除以 100 乘以 建议分量 除以 每日该宏量素总 g 数。结果以进度条或百分比数字展示。

食物列表按后端返回的 sort_order 排序展示。

小标题 1：Why Count Macros Instead of Calories?
正文：Calories tell you how much to eat. Macros tell you what to eat. A macro calculator breaks your daily intake into protein, carbs and fat — so you know your body is getting the right fuel, not just the right number. Whether your goal is weight loss, muscle gain, or maintenance, tracking macros gives you an actionable plan.

小标题 2：How This Calculator Works
三点：
其一，Enter your age, weight, height, activity level and goal — it takes under 30 seconds.
其二，We calculate your BMR and TDEE using the Mifflin-St Jeor equation, then split your daily calories into protein, carbs and fat based on your goal.
其三，Pick your country and see food suggestions — rice, noodles, chicken, eggs, nuts. Know exactly what to eat to hit every macro target.

小标题 3：More Than Just Numbers
正文：Most macro calculators stop at grams. Ours gives you food suggestions tailored to your country. Eating in China? We recommend rice, steamed buns and tofu. In the United States? Bread, potatoes and avocados. Your macros are universal — your meals are local.

第四段：FAQ（对应 FAQPage 结构化数据）
五个问题与答案，均 SSR 渲染：

问题 1：How much protein do I need per day?
答案：Most adults need 1.6 to 2.2 grams of protein per kilogram of body weight, depending on your activity level and goal. Our calculator uses your body weight and goal to give you a personalized number.

问题 2：How many carbs should I eat per day?
答案：Carb intake depends on your remaining calories after protein and fat are accounted for. For most people, carbs make up 40 to 60 percent of daily calories. Enter your stats above and our calculator does the math for you.

问题 3：What is a macro calculator?
答案：A macro calculator estimates how many grams of protein, carbohydrates and fat you should eat each day, based on your body stats, activity level and fitness goal. It turns your calorie target into an actionable eating plan.

问题 4：Is tracking macros better than counting calories?
答案：Counting calories helps you control how much you eat. Tracking macros also controls what you eat. For body composition goals like building muscle or losing fat while keeping muscle, macros give you more precise control.

问题 5：Do I need to track macros every day?
答案：Not necessarily. Once you use our calculator a few times and learn what a typical day of eating looks like for your goals, you can estimate without tracking every gram. Use it as a starting point and check back when your weight or goals change.
